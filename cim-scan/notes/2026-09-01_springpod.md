- **What they did:** "Gross margin expands from 69% to 85% ... driven by the Studio through lower freelance spend, faster turnaround and more reuse of content", with cost of revenue broadly flat while revenue doubles; "AI produces a first draft within hours of a completed employer brief, compared with weeks".
- **Evidence:** Checkable: COGS lines by year and the named driver. Missing: no isolated sizing, all forecast, and the Studio opened to students only in mid-October 2026.
- **Read:** Small and early, but the same content-cost curve as Madecraft, from a second seller in the same quarter.
- **Relevant portcos:** Career Certified, because a seller underwriting flat content cost against doubling revenue is the commoditization curve it is exposed to
- **Question:** Career Certified: does an AI-draft-then-edit workflow move its course build time anything like weeks to hours?
