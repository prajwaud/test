- **What it is:** Oil & gas / energy workforce training and competency management -
  PF revenue forecast to $30.0mm by 2031 (17% CAGR since 2023), PF Adj. EBITDA $0.9mm
  2023A to $3.1mm 2025A, forecast $11.3mm by 2031P (38% CAGR).
- **What they did:** Booked AI as a discrete revenue line in the forecast:
  "AI-enabled Operational Excellence Programs scaling from $0.5mm to $4.7mm" is named
  first among the key growth initiatives behind the 2031 plan - roughly 9x growth on a
  priced SKU, not a capability slide. Product vision recasts competency management as
  an "AI orchestration engine" with AI-driven targeted learning, and the development
  program ships a named AI coaching agent, NAVAGANT.
- **Evidence:** The AI program line sits inside the PF revenue build - Tier 4 on the
  in-the-numbers test. Separately, the market sizing leans on AI itself: data-center
  electricity "projected to nearly double by 2030," accelerated AI servers consuming
  "30% more power each year," argued as a driver of oil & gas training demand
  (ai_in_market_sizing). IHRDC also licensed a global power-generation client's job
  competency models - the models themselves are being monetized.
- **Read:** A training company pricing AI programs as a separate line rather than
  claiming internal efficiency. The 9x ramp on a $0.5mm base is the soft spot - one
  anchor client would make or break it.
- **Relevant portcos:**
  - Career Certified - because Houlihan Lokey's AI Readiness Report for it
    (2026-01-28, in the Vault) shows its bankers already scoring AI posture; IHRDC
    demonstrates the next step sellers will take - a priced AI program line, not a
    readiness narrative.
  - HSI - because its workplace training and compliance content library (Helix
    materials, Mar 2026, in the Vault) is the same asset class IHRDC is converting
    into "AI orchestration"; the question of curating versus publishing content is
    identical.
  - Talogy - because competency models are its core IP, and IHRDC licensing a
    client's competency models shows those models trading as standalone assets in
    the AI era.
- **Question:** IHRDC prices AI-enabled programs as a separate forecast line scaling
  ~9x. Do Career Certified or HSI have a priced AI SKU anywhere in plan, or is AI
  still bundled invisibly into existing content - and which position does a buyer
  reward at exit?
