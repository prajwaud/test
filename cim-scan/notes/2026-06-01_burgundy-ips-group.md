- **What it is:** Smart parking hardware, payments processing and curbside management
  for municipalities - 300K+ managed spaces, 550+ customers, 247 patents, pitching a
  hardware-to-software-first transition.
- **What they did:** Positioned AI as platform capability: Populus Elevate, a
  next-generation dashboard with "real-time dashboards, predictive analytics, and
  AI-enabled scenario modeling to optimize revenue," plus AI-driven LPR and vehicle
  sensors. The economics of the deck (margin, operating leverage) run on automation of
  manufacturing and payments attach, not on AI.
- **Evidence:** Product-level claims tied to named platforms and an acquisition
  (Populus) that brought the analytics layer. No AI economics anywhere: no efficiency
  numbers, no AI line in any bridge. The software-first transition is asserted through
  R&D staffing and patent counts.
- **Read:** A hardware install-base company buying/building an analytics layer and
  selling the data story as multiple expansion. The claim shape matters more than the
  vertical.
