- **What it is:** Real estate and financial education community operator - net
  revenue $150M 2025A growing to $257M 2029E, PF Adj. Cash EBITDA margins 28% to 50%
  by 2029, QoE by RSM.
- **What they did:** Built a member-facing AI monetization story around a proprietary
  platform, MALLI: MALLI Pro, a premium subscription AI tier with a $500K 2026 revenue
  target and roughly $2.0M of yearly high-margin revenue expected at rollout; MALLI
  Max, 575 Mac Mini M4 units deployed with a dedicated AI instance per flagship
  member; MALLI Connect, a "hivemind" network-matching engine routing member deal flow
  to capital partners; and a Q4 2026 "Agentic Autonomy" milestone - LOI Blaster, Deal
  Sauce and Prop AI connected via MCP so "MALLI executes the full deal pipeline
  autonomously."
- **Evidence:** The AI revenue targets are quantified but explicitly labeled "upside
  to budget" - not underwritten in the base case, which is what holds this at Tier 3
  rather than 4. Separately, a machine-learning model grounds the probability-weighted
  forward lead pipeline the near-term revenue visibility rests on. The EBITDA margin
  expansion is attributed to operating leverage and mix, not AI.
- **Read:** The inversion is the story: AI priced as a member-facing premium product
  (with dedicated hardware as the retention hook) rather than claimed as internal
  efficiency. Discipline point in its favor - the seller kept AI out of the base
  numbers and labeled it upside.
- **Relevant portcos:**
  - Career Certified - because real estate education is its core market, and New
    Reach is live evidence of what that customer base will pay for AI: a premium
    subscription tier, not a bundled feature.
  - TeamSnap - because it monetizes a consumer community with premium tiers, and
    MALLI Pro is a test case for AI-tier pricing in a passion-community context.
- **Question:** New Reach prices AI as a ~$2M member-facing premium subscription and
  keeps it out of the base case. Is there a premium AI tier worth piloting at Career
  Certified - and if so, does it get budgeted as upside the way a disciplined seller
  would present it?
