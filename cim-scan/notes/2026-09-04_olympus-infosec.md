- **What it is:** Cybersecurity certification training carve-out from Cengage Group -
  live and self-paced boot camps mapped to third-party credentials.
- **What they did:** Productized AI as syllabus, not as operating leverage: a 5-day
  "Generative and Agentic AI for Cybersecurity Professionals" boot camp, "Generative
  AI for Organizational Leaders," the ISACA Advanced in AI Security Management (AAISM)
  boot camp, and EC-Council's Certified Offensive AI Security Pro (C|OASP). AI-security
  credentials are presented as a growth SKU class.
- **Evidence:** AI appears as course catalog and market demand, with no AI-attributed
  internal economics in the retrieved sections. The notable fact is external: ISACA
  and EC-Council have already minted AI-security credentials, meaning the
  certification bodies - the industry's pricing anchors - have productized AI. Tier 2
  on named-product substantiation.
- **Read:** In professional training, AI is arriving as a certifiable credential
  before it arrives as a margin claim. Whoever holds credential distribution captures
  the demand wave without model risk.
- **Relevant portcos:**
  - HSI - because it sells workplace safety and compliance training into the same
    corporate L&D budgets, and AI-security / AI-governance credentials are the
    adjacent SKU class now moving; licensing an established credential beats building
    one.
  - Career Certified - because certification prep is its engine, and new AI
    credentials from recognized bodies (ISACA, EC-Council) are new prep markets with
    built-in demand.
- **Question:** ISACA and EC-Council have already productized AI-security
  credentials. Should HSI or Career Certified attach to existing AI credentials as
  prep and delivery partners now, rather than build proprietary AI curricula later?
