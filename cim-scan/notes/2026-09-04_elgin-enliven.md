- **What it is:** Pharmacy technology platform (patient engagement, workflow
  automation, financial experience) - PF Adj. EBITDA margin 15% 2026 to 30% 2031P,
  gross margin 59% Q1 2026 to 68% 2031P.
- **What they did:** Positioned an "AI Strategy: Building the Intelligent
  Orchestration Layer for Pharmacy" - next-best-action guidance for pharmacy teams,
  automation across adherence, refill, billing, reconciliation and outreach
  workflows, plus a responsible-AI frame for regulated healthcare (HIPAA-aware
  governance, approved tools, human oversight). "Continued AI & Automation" is one of
  five stated outcome pillars.
- **Evidence:** The margin bridge is deliberately not an AI story: 15% to 30% is
  attributed to gross margin lift (Insights & Outcomes mix 8% to 16% of revenue at
  75%+ GM), AWS operating leverage, and IVR replacement. The $3.5M of Q3 2025
  run-rate cost savings is attributed to a RIF and integration synergies with no AI
  attribution. AI stays at the capability layer with named workflows and no numbers -
  Tier 2 as cleanly as it comes.
- **Read:** A seller with a real automation platform choosing not to put AI in the
  numbers. Notable as the counter-example to Marketron in the same cohort.
- **Relevant portcos:**
  - Apotheco - because adherence, refill, billing and outreach workflows are the
    daily operating work of a specialty dermatology pharmacy; Enliven is selling the
    orchestration layer for exactly those tasks, which makes it either a vendor to
    evaluate or a preview of competitors' tooling.
  - PromptCare - because adherence and reconciliation automation map directly onto
    specialty home infusion operations.
- **Question:** Enliven sells workflow AI to pharmacies. Does Apotheco's plan assume
  any of these workflow gains today - and if a category vendor can deliver them, is
  build-vs-buy on pharmacy AI a question Apotheco has formally answered?
