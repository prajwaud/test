- **What it is:** European impact-management SaaS (grants, sponsorship, volunteering,
  ESG workflows) - €8.2M ARR 2026E, 97% NRR, EBITDA turning positive 2025, 15% margin
  2027E.
- **What they did:** Presented an engineering AI-adoption dashboard as a diligence
  exhibit: 45% of committed code AI-assisted with a stated objective of 100% by early
  Q3; ~2 days per week of AI-driven productivity gain per engineer; 100% of the team
  using AI multiple times daily; 70% rated advanced or expert. The adoption journey is
  dated and specific: Q3 2025 pilot (4 engineers, PHP/Laravel modernization), Q4 2025
  vendor benchmark across Claude, Augment Code, Gemini and ChatGPT with Claude
  selected, company-wide rollout early 2026 extended to product, support and lead
  generation, first agentic product capabilities targeted Q2 2026.
- **Evidence:** Quantified, current-state operational metrics - but the financial
  forecast runs on the ARR pipeline; no AI line appears in the P&L bridge, which is
  what holds this at Tier 3. Beyond engineering: an exploratory MCP track to expose
  platform capabilities into customers' agentic architectures, a quarterly AI product
  roadmap (2Q26-1Q27), and a governance stack (CISO-accountable generative AI policy,
  AI integrated into the ISO 27001 ISMS with 2026 certification audit, EU AI Act
  mapping scoped).
- **Read:** The most granular engineering AI-adoption disclosure seen in this scan to
  date. Sellers are learning to make AI adoption legible to buyers - dated pilots,
  named vendors, percentage metrics - without overreaching into the forecast.
- **Relevant portcos:**
  - PracticeTek, TeamSnap, Science Exchange - because these are WCP's software
    engineering organizations, and Optimy defines what a prepared seller's AI
    exhibit now looks like; Career Certified's AI Readiness Report (HL, 2026-01-28,
    in the Vault) confirms bankers are already scoring buyers' and sellers' AI
    posture in WCP's own processes.
- **Question:** If a buyer asked any WCP software portco for Optimy's four numbers
  today - % of committed code AI-assisted, productivity gain per engineer, % of team
  using AI daily, % rated advanced - could it produce them, and would we like the
  answer?
