- **What it is:** Vertical software for broadcast advertising - four product lines
  (Traffic, Digital, TaaS, Payments), ~$26M 2026B revenue, $7.0M 2026B Adj. EBITDA
  (+26% YoY), 68%+ gross margin, 94-95% NRR.
- **What they did:** Put AI inside the margin bridge. The stated path to a 35.1% Adj.
  EBITDA margin by 2028P is "driven by TaaS margin expansion, AI / automation-driven
  operating leverage, and continued revenue mix shift, each already in active
  execution." The TaaS engine is explicitly AI-staffed: "AI agents automate routine
  tasks within the hybrid onshore plus offshore workflow," with an "agentic onboarding
  layer" that trains new resources and self-updates the knowledge base, claiming
  declining time-to-productivity per new FTE. TaaS is sized as a ~$50M revenue
  opportunity within ~4,000 eligible stations.
- **Evidence:** AI is a named driver of the forecast margin path, not a roadmap
  garnish - that is the Tier 4 boundary. Gross margin expansion (~500bps 2023-2026B,
  to 71.2% by 2028P) is attributed to mix and TaaS delivery efficiency. Phased
  modernization roadmap (Q2 2026 - Q1 2027) pairs API work with "AI-driven automation
  of workflows." No third-party validation of the agent productivity claims was found
  in the retrieved sections; the QoE trail covers the financials, not the AI
  mechanics.
- **Read:** The cleanest example yet of a seller underwriting agentic labor into a
  services margin. If diligence can't decompose how much of the 2028P margin depends
  on agent productivity versus mix, the AI line is un-testable - and that is the
  question a buyer should force.
- **Relevant portcos:**
  - PracticeTek - because it runs the same model Marketron is pricing: vertical SaaS
    plus integrated payments, where a tech-enabled service tier is the next attach.
    Marketron is showing what bankers will accept as an "AI-staffed service line" story.
  - TeamSnap - because its advertising and sponsorship revenue sits on ad-workflow
    tooling of exactly the kind Marketron automates; the deck is a preview of the ad-ops
    cost curve competitors will claim.
- **Question:** Which WCP vertical-software portco could underwrite an AI-staffed
  service tier as a forecast revenue or margin line today - and what evidence would we
  require in our own plans before crediting it, given sellers are now crediting it with
  none?
