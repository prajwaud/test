- **What it is:** Managed operations and engineering services for Microsoft, Amazon and others, 2026E revenue $28.5M, 93.4% anchor concentration.
- **What they did:** An "AI-Attributed Revenue" series sits in the projection exhibit: 3.0% of revenue in FY23 to 40.6% in FY26 YTD, attributed by purchase order under "three rules", one of them an allocation of team hours.
- **Evidence:** Checkable: the figures reconcile to revenue and the method is disclosed. Missing: the definition is the seller's own, and the jump to 40.6% coincides with total revenue falling. A boundary call: this is a revenue mix, not an efficiency.
- **Read:** A template for relabelling services revenue as AI, and a diligence trap.
- **Relevant portcos:** Peritia, because an hours-allocation rule could relabel a share of its revenue the same way
- **Question:** Peritia: what share of FY26 purchase orders would qualify under Teleion's three rules, and would management want to defend that number?
