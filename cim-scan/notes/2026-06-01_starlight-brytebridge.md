- **What it is:** Compliance filings and recurring back-office services (state filings,
  annual reports, nonprofit formation) for SMBs and nonprofits.
- **What they did:** Built the whole growth story on an agentic stack: a proprietary
  first-party data acquisition engine, an "agentic marketing platform under exclusive
  arrangement" pushing millions of personalized sends per day, proprietary bots for
  state-level filings, and a stated intent to "continue to replace manual, non-client
  facing roles with autonomous AI engines" with "zero incremental headcount growth,
  regardless of top-line expansion."
- **Evidence:** The architecture is described in unusual operational detail (current vs
  future roadmap per stack layer, deliverability benchmarks claimed against industry).
  What is absent is any number: no CAC figure, no realized savings, no headcount
  baseline, no pilot results. Every economic claim is directional.
- **Read:** The headcount language is the tag to watch - this is the explicit
  "AI replaces roles" claim the scan flags as the leading indicator, made here as a
  forward promise with no realized quantification. Vocabulary has fully shifted:
  "agentic" is now load-bearing in a services CIM.
- **Relevant portcos:**
  - Career Certified - same CAC-driven B2C compliance-adjacent model, and the KB names
    AI content commoditization as its core risk. Is an agentic acquisition engine of
    this shape a threat, a playbook, or both?
  - HSI - compliance content and lifecycle marketing at 27K+ customers; the
    cross-sell/renewal orchestration claims map directly.
  - TeamSnap - lifecycle marketing automation is on its roadmap, and the KB flags
    over-automation degrading lead quality as a named risk; BryteBridge is the
    aggressive end of that spectrum.
