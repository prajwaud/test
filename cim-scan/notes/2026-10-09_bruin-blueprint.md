- **What it is:** Healthcare exam prep carve-out (Rosh Review for physicians and PAs,
  NP Reviews for nursing) - FY26E revenue $28.5M, business-unit EBITDA $15.9M (55.7%)
  growing to $26.2M (57.5%) in FY29P on an FTI carve-out cost basis; ~47K medical and
  ~29K PA/nursing learners; B2B mix rising.
- **What they did:** Quantified AI in content production with a clinician in the loop:
  "4,500+ Questions Drafted by AI," "~100% AI-Assisted, Clinician-Validated," "4,925+
  Estimated Hours Saved." Markets "the only AI tutor powered by Socratic learning,"
  and generative branching clinical case simulations scored in real time (proof of
  concept today, next steps H2 2026). A migration to a new LMS in H1 2027 is stated to
  "unlock significantly enhanced personalization, AI functionality" for the GME line.
- **Evidence:** Current-state operating metrics, not forecast drivers - the roadmap to
  $45M sales and $26M EBITDA is attributed to Nursing, Transition-to-Practice and EMS
  offerings, and the content opex line grows from $2.3M to $3.3M over the plan rather
  than falling. That holds it at Tier 3.
- **Read:** Disciplined use of AI where it is most defensible in regulated education:
  AI drafts, clinicians validate, and the deck counts the hours.
- **Relevant portcos:**
  - Career Certified - because certification exam prep is its engine, and Blueprint
    shows the production line buyers will now expect to see quantified: questions
    drafted by AI, hours saved, and a documented expert-validation step.
  - HSI - because its continuing-education and certification content faces the same
    accuracy bar, where a validation step is the difference between an efficiency
    claim and a liability.
- **Question:** Blueprint reports hours saved per AI-drafted question and keeps a
  clinician in the loop. Does Career Certified track an equivalent metric on its own
  question-bank production - and is its validation step documented well enough to
  survive the same diligence?
