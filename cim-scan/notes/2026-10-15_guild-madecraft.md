- **What they did:** The P&L commentary attributes gross margin expansion (24% to 82%, FY23A-FY29P) to an "optimized production model driven by AI efficiency gains and the author-generated content production". Footnote: "Per management, AI tooling and a film-at-home model have reduced production headcount needs by ~50%." Cost per course falls from "$6.0 - 7.5K" to "$0.95K" for author-generated.
- **Evidence:** Checkable: an itemized per-course cost build. Missing: AI is not separated from the film-at-home format shift, which carries most of the cost cut; the headcount figure is a management assertion with no FTE count.

- **Relevant portcos:** Career Certified, because its named risk is AI-native competitors replicating course content at lower cost; HSI, because content-production leverage is part of its margin story
