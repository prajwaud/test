- **What it is:** Medical transcription, clinical documentation, coding and RCM services for US and ANZ providers, delivered mainly from India (1,237 billable employees, 1,026 in India), repositioning as an AI platform (TrueRCM, Rapid Coding AI, RapidNote scribe).
- **What they did:** A table headed "AI Led Platformization & Cost Optimization Plan - Impact on EBITDA Margin (FY27-28)" sizes AI rows in margin points: White AI "driving per FTE throughput from ~220 pages/ ~500 pages/day" with a "reduction of delivery base by up to 40%" (targeted 1-2 points), Rapid Coding automation (1-2 points), and migration onto TrueRCM at "80 vis-a-vis 50-60%" margins (2-3 points). The roadmap adds "TrueRCM AI platform to automate 35-45% of back-end RCM operations" and a revenue per FTE target on the platform.
- **Evidence:** Checkable: AI is a named line in a margin plan with point ranges, and the per-FTE throughput figure is the company's own. Missing: no dollar base and no realized-versus-targeted split; the AI rows sit beside offshoring rows ("60-70% cost reduction per resource migration") and the two levers are not separated. Several figures are garbled in the index text and need checking against the PDF.
- **Read:** The first healthcare services CIM in this corpus to put AI into its margin plan, and to tie a delivery headcount cut to it. A buyer will not be able to tell AI from labor arbitrage in these numbers; the next sellers in RCM will have to.
- **Relevant portcos:** Fusion Health, because its coding QA and audits initiative is sized on coder throughput, and this seller quotes ~220 to ~500 pages a day per FTE as the AI effect; PromptCare, because RCM automation (denials, DSO) is its lever before exit, and an outcome-priced RCM vendor quoting 80% margins is both a vendor option and a comp; UVP, because RCM is named AI scope for its exit narrative, and buyers will now see sellers quoting per-FTE throughput
